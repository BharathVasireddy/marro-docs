---
title: Delete your account
description: Delete your own Marro login, on the web or from the app.
---

Open **Your account** from the menu under your name. In the app, go to **Account → Delete account**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your password" />
  <Step title="Tick the confirmation" />
  <Step title="Choose Delete my account" />
</Steps>

## What happens

| Your data | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Organizations | You leave every one and are signed out everywhere |
| Name, email, phone | Removed. Connected email and calendars are disconnected |
| Leads, notes, other work | Stay with each organization, without your name |
| Your login | Gone for good. To use Marro again you need a new invitation |

## Sort these out first

The page tells you which applies.

| If | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| You own an organization | [Hand it to another admin or close it](/guides/organisation-closure) |
| You are the only admin of an organization | Make someone else an admin there first |
| You are a Marro platform administrator | Another platform Owner must remove that role |
| You sign in with Google or Microsoft and have no password | Set one from the sign-in page first |

Or email [support](https://app.marro.si/legal/support) from your sign-in address, and we delete the account within 30 days.

<Note>
Deleting your account is not closing your organization. Closing removes everyone's data and only the owner can do it.
</Note>
