---
title: Delete your account
description: Delete your own Marro login, on the web or from the app.
---

Open **Your account** from the menu under your name (in the app: **Account → Delete account**). Enter your password, tick the confirmation and choose **Delete my account**.

What happens:

- You leave every organisation and are signed out everywhere, on the web and in the app.
- Your name, email address and phone number are removed. Connected email and calendars are disconnected.
- Leads, notes and other work you did stay with each organisation, without your name.
- It cannot be undone. To use Marro again you need a new invitation.

Some things need sorting out first, and the page tells you which:

- **You own an organisation.** [Hand it to another admin or close it](/guides/organisation-closure).
- **You are the only admin of an organisation.** Make someone else an admin there first.
- **You are a Marro platform administrator.** Another platform Owner must remove that role.

If you sign in with Google or Microsoft and have no password, set one from the sign-in page first. You can also email [support](https://app.marro.si/legal/support) from the address you sign in with, and we delete the account within 30 days.

Deleting your account is not the same as closing your organisation: that removes everyone's data and only the owner can do it.
