The Marro app puts your work on your phone. Administration and settings stay on the web.

## Install it

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. On your Android phone (Android 8 or newer), open [app.marro.si/app](https://app.marro.si/app) and tap **Download Marro**.
    2. If Android asks, allow Chrome to install the app.
    3. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**.

    Install Marro only from that page. The Google Play listing is in review.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    The iPhone app is in App Store review. Until then, use Marro in Safari at [app.marro.si](https://app.marro.si).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your phone number">
    Use the number on your Marro account and tap **Send code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the 6-digit code">
    It arrives on WhatsApp. If it doesn't, wait for the timer and tap **Email it to me**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Prefer a password? Tap **Use email instead**. If you work in several organizations, the app asks which one.

## Work your leads

| Do this | To |
| --- | --- |
| Tap a stage pill | Narrow the list to that stage |
| Tap the **Favourites** pill | Show only your starred leads |
| Swipe a card right | Call |
| Swipe a card left | WhatsApp |
| Long press | Select, for bulk actions |
| Pull down | Refresh |

Open a lead and choose **Activity** to see its history by day. Filter by **Notes**, **Calls**, **Messages** or **Updates**. **⋯** has **Add a note** and **Send email**.

## Inbox tips

| Do this | To |
| --- | --- |
| Press and hold a WhatsApp message | Reply to it, react or copy |
| Tap the magnifier | Search within the chat |

## Approve HR requests

If your role approves HR requests, each leave, regularisation or timesheet waits as a card.

| Do this | To |
| --- | --- |
| Swipe right | Approve (asks first) |
| Swipe left | Reject (asks for a reason, which the person sees) |
| Tap the card | See balances, who else is off, check-ins or hours |
| **Undo** within 15 minutes | Put a decided request back to waiting |

You get a notification when something is waiting.

## Calls and recordings

- **Menu → Calls** lists AI, Cloud and Bridge calls. An AI call opens its **Recording & transcript**.
- **Listen to recording** plays the audio when it is available.
- For calls through MCube or another calling integration, **Transcribe recording** creates a transcript if your organization has it.
- Android installed from Marro's page can show which lead is calling before you answer.

## Notifications

They never include the words of a message. Tap one to open its chat, follow-up, appointment, support request or HR request. A notification for another workspace opens **Account** so you can choose.

## Offline

A customer record added or changed with no signal waits on your phone and is sent when you are back online. Record-link custom fields and deleting records stay on the web.
