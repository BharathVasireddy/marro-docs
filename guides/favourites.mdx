---
title: Favourites
description: Keep your records and saved views in your sidebar.
---

Choose **Favourite** on a lead, contact, company or opportunity page. On a list, open
a saved view and choose **Favourite** beside its Views control.

Your **Favourites** section appears in the sidebar once there is something to show.
It belongs only to you in the organization you are using. Other users have their own
favourites, and switching organization opens that organization's set.

Open a favourite's menu to **Move up**, **Move down** or **Remove favourite**. These
controls work with a keyboard. Removing a favourite does not delete its record or view.
You can keep up to 50 favourites in each organization.

The sidebar uses the record's current name. Deleted records, archived leads, removed
views and targets you can no longer access disappear. A favourite never grants access
to a record or another user's private view. When there are no visible favourites, the
section is hidden. Before the database update is applied, the Favourite control says
it is not available yet and the existing sidebar continues to work.

Ask Marro or the staff WhatsApp agent to list your favourites, add or remove one, or
change their order. Changes use the usual approval and the same access checks as the
web. Names are record data, never instructions to the assistant.

## In the mobile app

Tap the **star** in the header of a lead, contact, company or opportunity to add it to your
favourites, and tap it again to remove it. Your favourites are under **Account** > **Favourites**:
tap one to open it, swipe left to remove it, or choose **Arrange** to move rows (tap a row to move
it up, swipe right to move it down). The list is the same as on the web. Saved views and custom
records you starred on the web are listed there but open on the web; the app does not have a star
for them yet. This needs the app version that includes favourites.
