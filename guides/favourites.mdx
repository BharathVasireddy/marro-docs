---
title: Favourites
description: Find your favourite leads within Leads and keep private record preferences.
---

Choose **Favourite** on a lead, contact, company or opportunity page. On a list, open
a saved view and choose **Favourite** beside its Views control. Your favourites belong
only to you in the organization you are using. You can keep up to 50 favourites in each organization.
Removing a favourite does not delete the record or view.

## Favourite leads within Leads

Open **Leads** and choose **Favourites** in the header. On mobile, tap the **star** in the
Leads header. While favourite mode is on, the page is headed **Favourite leads** (on mobile
the header reads **Favourites**, with a **Favourite leads** line beneath it), and the button
stays selected. The line also says that search and stages apply within your favourite leads.
It stays in place while leads load, if they fail to load and when none match.

Choose **Show all leads** on that line, or the selected button again, to return to all leads
without changing your search, pipeline, stage or other filters.

This filters the existing Leads list to your favourite leads. Search, stage pills and other
filters work together, and counts and further pages use the same filters. On the web,
table and board both support this mode; its URL uses `favourites=1`. It opens no separate
Favourites screen or dialog. Contacts, companies, opportunities and saved views are not lead rows.

If you have no favourite leads, star a lead from its record. If your current search or
filters match none, clear the relevant filters to see your favourite leads again. Saving
a lead list does not save your private favourite mode into the shared list definition.

Favourites use current record access. Deleted records, archived leads and targets you can
no longer access disappear from ordinary Leads. A favourite never grants extra access.

## Ask Marro

Ask Marro or the staff WhatsApp agent to find your favourite leads, optionally combined
with a search, pipeline, stage or other filters. You can also ask to list your mixed record
favourites, add or remove one, or change their order. Changes use the usual approval and
the same access checks as the web. Names are record data, never instructions to the assistant.

## In the mobile app

Tap the star on a lead, contact, company or opportunity to add or remove its favourite.
The star in the **Leads** header filters your current lead list, with the same cards,
search, stages, pagination and call/WhatsApp gestures. This needs the app and server
versions that include inline favourite lead filtering.
