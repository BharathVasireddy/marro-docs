---
title: Favourites
description: Star the records you use most and find your favourite leads quickly.
---

Favourites are private to you. You can keep up to 50 in each organization. Removing one does not delete the record, and a favourite never gives you access to anything you could not already see.

## Add a favourite

<Steps>
  <Step title="On a record">
    Choose **Favourite** on a lead, contact, company or opportunity page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On a list">
    Open a saved view and choose **Favourite** beside **Views**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Show only your favourite leads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Leads">
    Open **Leads**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Favourites">
    It is in the header on the web, beside the pipeline on a phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back">
    Choose it again to see all leads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Search, stage and other filters still work with it, on both table and board. No favourites yet? Star a lead first. Seeing none with filters on? Clear the filters.

## In the mobile app

| Where | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Star on a record | Adds or removes the favourite |
| Star in the **Leads** header | Filters your lead list, with the same search, stages and gestures |
