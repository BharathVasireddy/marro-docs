---
title: Favourites
description: Keep your records and saved views one click away from Leads.
---

Choose **Favourite** on a lead, contact, company or opportunity page. On a list, open
a saved view and choose **Favourite** beside its Views control.

Open **Leads** and choose **Favourites** in the header (the star beside the search on a
phone) to see them. Search by name or a lead's phone number, and choose a stage to see
only the favourite leads in that stage; **All favourites** shows every kind again. Your
favourites belong only to you in the organization you are using. Other users have their own
favourites, and switching organization opens that organization's set.

Open a favourite's menu to **Move up**, **Move down** or **Remove favourite**. These
controls work with a keyboard. Moving waits until the search and stage are cleared, so
the order you save is always the whole list. Removing a favourite does not delete its record or view.
You can keep up to 50 favourites in each organization.

Favourites use the record's current name, and a lead's current stage and number. Deleted records, archived leads, removed
views and targets you can no longer access disappear. A favourite never grants access
to a record or another user's private view. Before the database update is applied, the
Favourite control says it is not available yet.

Ask Marro or the staff WhatsApp agent to list your favourites, add or remove one, or
change their order. Changes use the usual approval and the same access checks as the
web. Names are record data, never instructions to the assistant.

## In the mobile app

Tap the **star** in the header of a lead, contact, company or opportunity to add it to your
favourites, and tap it again to remove it. Open **Leads** and tap the **star** in the header to see
them: tap one to open it, swipe left to remove it, or choose **Arrange** to move rows (tap a row to move
it up, swipe right to move it down). Search by name or a lead's number, and tap a stage to see only
the favourite leads in it; **Arrange** waits until the search and stage are cleared. Searching by
number and choosing a stage need the server update that sends each lead's stage; until then the
app searches names only. The list is the same as on the web. Saved views and custom
records you starred on the web are listed there but open on the web; the app does not have a star
for them yet. This needs the app version that includes favourites.
