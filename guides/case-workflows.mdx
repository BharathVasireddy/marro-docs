A workflow is a checklist on an opportunity or case. It uses the record's stage and owner, your contacts, task boards and document requests. It doesn't move stages or run automations on its own.

## Set up a case

Open an opportunity and find **Workflow**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add participants">From your contacts, with a role such as client, applicant or adviser. A role gives no access to the case or its files.</Step>
  <Step title="Add requirement">Say what is needed, and pick **Task** or **Document**.</Step>
  <Step title="Link it">Link an existing task or document request, or leave it unlinked while planning. It stays outstanding until linked work is complete.</Step>
  <Step title="Mark optional">Nonessential work is left out of the progress count.</Step>
</Steps>

Once linked, a task keeps its own owner and deadline, and a document keeps its request's deadline. Each row's menu has **Edit requirement** and **Open task** or **Open document**.

Document requests can only be linked from the case's connected original lead, and you need access to that lead. Create and review the requests there.

## Read progress

| Item | Counts as done when |
| --- | --- |
| Task | Its board stage is marked complete |
| Document | The request is accepted (an upload still needs review) |
| Optional item | Never counted |

Changes requested, overdue work, missing owners and unlinked requirements stay visible.

Finishing the checklist doesn't advance the case, send a message or collect payment. Staff make the professional decisions.

## Reuse a checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">On a case with no requirements, open **Workflow actions → Reuse checklist**.</Step>
  <Step title="Pick a case">Another case you can access.</Step>
  <Step title="Review and apply">Only the requirements are copied; participants, links, assignments and dates are cleared.</Step>
</Steps>
