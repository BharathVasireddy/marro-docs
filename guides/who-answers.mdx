Who answers is one list of rules that decides who gets a call. Open it in **Calls → Settings → Who answers**. You need to be an organization administrator.

## How the rules work

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calls/who-answers.jpg" alt="Who answers showing a published call flow in plain words" />
</Frame>

The first rule that matches the number called is used. The last rule covers every other call.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **If the call is to** | Pick numbers. Each number can ring a different team. |
| **The call goes to** | Staff, the AI agent, or nobody (a missed call). |
| **Ring** | A team, a [ring group](/guides/ring-groups), the lead’s owner or chosen people, and **Give up after**. |
| **Ring someone else first** | Adds a second step if nobody answers. |
| **Different outside working hours and on holidays** | Another handling for those times. Hours come from your HR working week and holidays. |

## Save your rules

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add or edit a rule">Pick the numbers, then who gets the call.</Step>
  <Step title="Fix anything shown at the bottom">For example a rule with no numbers, or ringing over five minutes.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Choose **Save and use these rules**.</Step>
</Steps>

People need active access and a registered phone number. Busy or away staff are skipped.
