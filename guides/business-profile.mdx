The business profile is what your business does, in your own words. Open **Settings → Business → Business profile**. The assistant and AI agents read it.

## Your business

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/setup/business-profile.jpg" alt="The Business profile page with industry, audience, offer, sales tracking and currency" />
</Frame>

| Setting | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Industry** | The closest match to your business |
| **You work with** | Who your customers are |
| **What you offer** | A sentence or two, in your own words |
| **Track sales separately** | Each sale gets its own value and stage |
| **Currency** | The currency you sell in |

## Industry starter

**Set up for your industry** adds a ready pipeline, fields and follow-ups. It only adds; nothing existing changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your industry">Open **Industry starter** and pick the closest one.</Step>
  <Step title="Review what will be added">Untick or edit the pipeline, fields, booking types and follow-ups.</Step>
  <Step title="Apply">Only what you leave ticked is added. Starter booking types and workflows must be enabled separately.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/leads/industry-starter.jpg" alt="Suggested fields for an industry" />
</Frame>

See also [Custom fields](/guides/custom-fields).

## Words and guidance

Under **Advanced → Words and guidance**, change the words you use for things, and tell the assistant how you work.

| Setting | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Words you use** | Your own names for records, shown to your team |
| **Before the next step** | What to learn about a lead first |
| **After a sale** | What happens once a lead converts |
| **How you look after customers** | Your aftercare, in a few lines |
