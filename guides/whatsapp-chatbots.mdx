A **chatbot** answers customers on WhatsApp step by step. It is part of the Inbox, and building or switching one on needs the **Inbox settings** permission.

## Build a chatbot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chatbots">
    Open **Inbox → Settings → Chatbots** and choose **New chatbot**. It starts with a small example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it starts">
    Type **keywords** separated by commas. A keyword matches a whole word in any case. You can also switch on **First message of a new chat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add steps">
    Each step does one thing and says which step comes next. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and switch on">
    The page lists anything to fix, such as a step that leads nowhere. **Save and switch on** works once nothing is left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step types

| Step | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Say something** | Sends a message |
| **Ask with buttons** | Up to three buttons, each leading to its own next step |
| **Ask with a list** | Up to ten options |
| **Ask for typed words** | A word, number, email address or phone number. Name the answer to reuse it |
| **Branch on an answer** | Picks the next step from an earlier answer |
| **Set a lead field** | Saves an answer, or words you write, into a lead field. A value the field refuses is never saved |
| **Hand over to a person**, **Hand over to the AI receptionist**, **End the chat** | Finishes the chatbot |

## How it behaves

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One at a time" icon="message">
    A chat runs at most one chatbot.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never over your team" icon="user">
    It does not start while a person or the AI receptionist has the chat, and stops when a person sends a message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="24-hour window" icon="clock">
    It runs only while WhatsApp allows free messages. If the window closes, it ends quietly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="STOP" icon="ban">
    A customer who replies STOP is never messaged again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If an answer is not accepted, it asks again once. A second miss follows the step's **Unclear answer** choice, or hands the chat to a person.

## See what it did

| Where | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Above the message box | Which chatbot ran, its state and the answers so far |
| **Stop** | Ends it so your team can take over |
| The lead | A note with the collected answers when it hands over or finishes |
