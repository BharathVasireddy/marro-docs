A **chatbot** answers customers on WhatsApp step by step. It is part of the Inbox, and building or switching one on needs the **Inbox settings** permission.

<Flow steps={["Keyword or first message", "Questions", "Lead fields filled", "Hand over or end"]} />

## Your chatbots

Open **Inbox → Settings → Chatbots**. Each chatbot is one row:

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Chatbot** | Its name, how many steps it has and whether it is still a draft |
| **On** | The switch. Off means customers never get its messages. Turning it off also stops chats it is running |
| **Starts on** | The keywords, or "new chats" |
| **Numbers** | The WhatsApp numbers it answers on, or "Every number" |
| **Used** | How many chats it has started (and how many are still going) |
| **Completed** | How many ran to the end (and how many were handed to a person or the AI receptionist) |
| **Last used** | When it last started a chat |

The **⋯** menu on a row has **Edit**, **Duplicate** (a draft copy you can change safely) and **Delete**. You can have as many chatbots as you like. When a customer's message matches several, the first one in the list answers, and a chat only ever runs one chatbot at a time.

## Build a chatbot on the canvas

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Choose **New chatbot**. It opens with a small example: a greeting, three buttons and a hand-over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it starts">
    Click the **Start** card. Add **keywords** (a keyword matches a whole word in any case), and/or switch on **A new chat's first message**. If you have more than one WhatsApp number, choose which numbers it answers on; with none chosen it answers on all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add steps">
    Drag a step from the list on the left onto the canvas, or click it to add it. On a phone use **Add a step**. A step added while another step is chosen joins to it automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join them with lines">
    Drag from the dot on the right of a step to the step that should come next. Let go on empty canvas to add a new step there, already joined. A step with several outcomes (buttons, list options, conditions) has one dot for each. Click a line to cut it, or use the **After this** list in the step's panel, which works well on a phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and switch on">
    The line under the name says what is left to fix, such as a step with no words or a button over WhatsApp's length. Click an item to jump to it. **Save**, then switch it on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Drag the background to move around, pinch or use the zoom buttons to zoom, **Tidy up** lays the steps out again, and Delete removes the chosen step. A map appears in the corner once there are many steps.

## Step types

| Step | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send a message** | Plain text |
| **Message with a link button** | A message with one button (up to 20 characters) that opens a web page (https) |
| **Ask with buttons** | Up to **3** reply buttons (20 characters each), each leading to its own next step |
| **Ask with a list** | A menu of up to **10** options (24 characters each, optional 72-character description) |
| **Ask a question** | A typed answer: any words, a number, an email address or a phone number. Name the answer to use it later |
| **Ask for a location** | The customer taps a button to share a pin, or types an address |
| **Send a form** | Opens one of your published WhatsApp forms (Flows) and goes on when the customer completes it |
| **Condition** | Picks the next step from an earlier answer (is exactly / contains) |
| **Wait** | Pauses from 1 minute to 23 hours, then goes on |
| **Update the lead** | Saves an answer, or words you write, into a lead field. A value the field refuses is never saved |
| **Add or remove a tag** | Tags the lead |
| **Assign to a team member** | Makes someone the lead's owner |
| **Call a web address** | Sends the answers (a JSON POST) to a public https address. If it fails, the chatbot still goes on |
| **Hand over to a person**, **Hand over to the AI receptionist**, **End** | Finishes the chatbot |

Choices and limits come from WhatsApp itself, and the builder shows a counter next to every text that has one. Templates and media or document cards are not steps yet: use a link button to open a document.

## How it behaves

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One at a time" icon="message">
    A chat runs at most one chatbot.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never over your team" icon="user">
    It does not start while a person or the AI receptionist has the chat, and stops when a person sends a message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="24-hour window" icon="clock">
    It runs only while WhatsApp allows free messages. If the window closes, even during a **Wait**, it ends quietly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="STOP" icon="ban">
    A customer who replies STOP is never messaged again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If an answer is not accepted, it asks again once. A second miss follows the step's **Not understood twice** line, or hands the chat to a person. While a chatbot is on a **Wait** step, what the customer writes does not change anything, and the AI receptionist and keyword replies stay quiet.

## See what it did

| Where | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Above the message box | Which chatbot ran, its state and the answers so far |
| **Stop** | Ends it so your team can take over |
| The lead | A note with the collected answers when it hands over or finishes |

## On a phone

The list becomes a stack of cards. The canvas works with touch: drag to move around, pinch to zoom, tap a step to open its panel from the bottom of the screen. The **After this** section of each step lets you choose what comes next without drawing a line.
