**Reports → Campaigns** counts the period's new leads by the campaign they came from, and shows what each cost and brought back.

## Where a lead's campaign comes from

<Flow steps={["Link with utm_campaign", "Form, ad or website", "Lead keeps campaign", "Counted in report"]} />

Every lead keeps where it **first** and **most recently** came in, with its campaign tags (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term`, `utm_content`). The lead page shows them under **Where they came from**. Meta and Google lead ads bring their own campaign name.

A visit to your website counts as where a lead came from too, when [website tracking](/guides/website-tracking) is on: it is the first when it came before the form, and a direct visit never counts as the most recent.

Add `utm_campaign=spring-intake` to links you share and those leads are counted under that campaign.

## Set up a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">Go to **Reports → Campaigns → Manage campaigns → New campaign**. It needs the **Manage reports** permission.</Step>
  <Step title="Name it">Leads with the same campaign tag, ignoring capitals, are counted in it. **Also matches** adds other names. A name belongs to one campaign.</Step>
  <Step title="Channel, dates, currency">The currency cannot change once spend is entered.</Step>
  <Step title="Spend by month">Type each month's spend, then **Save**. Leave a month empty to remove it.</Step>
</Steps>

Names leads carry that you have not set up show as **Not set up yet**, each with **Add**.

## Read the report

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New leads | Added in the period, by **first touch** (or **latest touch** with the switch) |
| Qualified | Past the first stage and not lost, plus converted |
| Converted | Now in a converted stage |
| Spend | What you entered, for the days of each month in the period |
| Cost per lead, per conversion | Spend divided by new or converted leads |
| Revenue | Payments marked paid by those leads |
| Return on spend | Revenue divided by spend: 3.2× means 3.2 paid per 1 spent |

Money is never added across currencies: other-currency payments are listed beneath. People who see only their own leads do not see spend. Spend is entered by hand; Meta and Google Ads spend is not automatic yet.
