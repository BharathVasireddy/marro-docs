title: "WhatsApp groups"
description: "Make a WhatsApp group from the Inbox, invite people with a link, and write to everyone in it."
---

**Inbox → Groups** lists the WhatsApp groups your business made. A group has its own thread, and its people are linked to leads where their phone number matches.

<Warning>
  Groups need a number that Meta allows to make them: an **Official Business Account** on the WhatsApp Cloud API. Numbers still used in the WhatsApp Business app, or added through Multi-solution Conversations, cannot. If yours does not qualify, Meta refuses when you create the group and Marro shows its reason.
</Warning>

## Limits

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Size | Up to **8 people** besides your business, which is always the admin |
| Joining | Through the **invite link** only; you can't add someone by number |
| Content | Text, pictures, videos and documents. No buttons or lists |
| Cost | Meta bills your business directly. Marro shows no prices |

## Make a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose New group">
    In **Inbox → Groups**. You need permission to manage WhatsApp settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Add a name (up to 128 characters), an optional description and the number. **I approve each person who asks to join** is on by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Copy the invite link and send it. **New link** makes a fresh one and stops every earlier one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a group

| Do this | How |
| --- | --- |
| Write to everyone | Type in the box under the messages |
| Approve people | Under **Waiting to join**, **Approve** or **Reject**. **Check for requests** reloads the list |
| Rename | **Edit** |
| Add existing groups | **Import existing groups** (up to 50 at a time; sends nothing) |
| Remove a person | **×** beside them. They can't rejoin with the link |
| Delete | **Delete** removes everyone and can't be undone. Marro keeps the history |

Replies show the person's name, with **· lead** next to numbers that match a lead. Marro never makes a lead from a group member.

## Who can use it

| Permission | Allows |
| --- | --- |
| See all WhatsApp chats | Reading and writing in groups |
| Manage WhatsApp settings | Creating, deleting, removing people, renewing the link |

## Ask the assistant

<Card title="What it can do" icon="comments">
List and read groups, create one, send a message, approve or reject people, rename, import and make a new invite link. Each change is shown first. Removing people and deleting are done on this page only.
</Card>

Not here yet: templates, broadcasts or automations into a group, sending files from the thread, changing a group's picture, and the mobile app.
