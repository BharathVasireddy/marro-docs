Customer quotes are the quotes made before quotes moved to Finance. They are listed in **Finance → Quotes** with every other quote, marked **Customer quote**. Make new quotes with **New quote** there, or on a lead or customer: see [Quotes and invoices](/guides/quotes-and-invoices).

<Note>
Publishing gives you a private link to share yourself. Marro does not send it. Certified e-signing is not configured.
</Note>

## Prepare a quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Use a draft or a template. Open existing ones from **Finance → Quotes** (archived under **Archived**) and templates under **Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supplier and customer">
    Enter the supplier name, address and tax identity from your confirmed details, then the customer, currency and validity date. Marro never supplies a GSTIN, tax treatment or legal terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lines">
    Each line has a description, quantity, unit amount, fixed discount and named tax amounts. Use **Tax not configured** when unsure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Review totals, validity and terms, then publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Marro does not calculate statutory tax. A discount cannot exceed its line. A linked payment is context only: no charge is made.

## Publish and decide

<Flow steps={["Draft", "Publish: private link", "Customer accepts or declines"]} />

- Publishing creates a fixed revision and a new private link. It sends nothing and takes no payment.
- Editing a published quote switches off its old link. Publish a new revision to replace it.
- Accepted, declined or cancelled quotes cannot be edited. Make a new quote.
- Expired links cannot record a decision.

## What acceptance means

| Accepting | |
| --- | --- |
| Records | A digital decision on that exact revision |
| The link | Does not prove who holds it |
| Is it e-signing? | **Not** certified e-signing |
| Legal enforceability | No claim is made |

## Cancel or archive

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Cancel** | Stops an open quote |
| **Archive** | Removes it from active lists and stops customer access. History and acceptance stay |

There is no delete. Deleting a linked contact, company, opportunity or payment archives the quote. Restoring it does not reopen the quote.

## Templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply a template">
    It gives you a draft of reusable lines and terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish it">
    Choose the customer and a fresh validity date, then review everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Templates belong to their owner, within your own, team or all permissions. Changing one never changes existing quotes.
