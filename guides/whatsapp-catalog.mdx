If your business has a catalog in Meta Commerce Manager, you can send products in a WhatsApp chat. Sending a catalog message charges and reserves nothing.

## Save your catalog

Products live in Commerce Manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    **Inbox → Settings → Commerce → Catalog and payments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the catalog">
    Enter the catalog ID and a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each number">
    Press **Check with Meta** to see, and change, whether the catalog button and carts are on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send products

In a chat, press the shopping bag beside the reply box. Within 24 hours of the customer's last message you can send:

| Send | Includes |
| --- | --- |
| **One product** | Optional text |
| **Several products** | A header, a text and up to 30 products in sections |
| **The catalog** | A text, and optionally a product whose picture is shown |

After 24 hours WhatsApp only allows a template. Customers who replied STOP are not messaged.

## Carts

<Flow steps={["Customer sends a cart", "Note on their lead, once"]} />

The note lists the products, quantities and any message. Prices are shown as Meta sends them.

## Ask for payment on WhatsApp

Off until an administrator turns it on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your gateway">
    In **Inbox → Settings → Catalog and payments → Payment requests**: Razorpay, PayU, BillDesk or Zaakpay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the configuration name">
    The name of the payment configuration made in WhatsApp Manager (Payments, India). Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch Allow payment requests on">
    Marro stores no keys. Payments are in rupees, up to ₹5,00,000.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To ask for payment, open a chat with a lead who has a sent invoice, press the shopping bag, choose **Payment request** and pick the invoice. Check the amount still due. Nothing sends until you press **Send payment request**. The customer sees Meta's **Review and Pay**.

- One open request per invoice. Cancel it before asking again. A paid request can't be canceled.
- **Marro never charges anything and does not mark the invoice paid.** Use **Record payment** once the money arrives.
