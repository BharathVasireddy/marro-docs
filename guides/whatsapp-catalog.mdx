---
title: "WhatsApp catalog"
description: "Send products from your Meta catalog in a chat, and see the carts customers send back."
---

If your business has a catalog in Meta Commerce Manager, you can send one product, several products or the whole catalog in a WhatsApp chat. When the customer sends a cart back, it appears as a note on their lead. Sending a catalog message charges and reserves nothing.

## Save your catalog

Products live in Commerce Manager. In **Inbox → Settings → Catalog and payments**, save the catalog's number (catalog ID) and a name. For each connected number, press **Check with Meta** to see, and change, whether the catalog button and carts are on. The assistant can save a catalog and change these too, after you approve.

## Send products

In a chat, press the shopping bag button beside the reply box and choose what to send. Within 24 hours of the customer's last message you can send:

- **One product**: the catalog and the product's Content ID, with an optional text.
- **Several products**: a header, a text and up to 30 products in sections.
- **The catalog**: a text, and optionally a product whose picture is shown.

Outside the 24 hours WhatsApp only allows a template. Customers who replied STOP are not messaged. Ask the assistant to send products and you approve the exact message first.

## Carts

A cart a customer sends is written on their lead once, with the products and quantities and any note they added. Prices are shown as Meta sends them.

## Ask for payment on WhatsApp

Payment requests are off until an administrator turns them on. In **Inbox → Settings → Catalog and payments → Payment requests**, choose your gateway (Razorpay, PayU, BillDesk or Zaakpay), enter the name of the payment configuration you made in WhatsApp Manager (Payments, India), save, then switch **Allow payment requests** on. Marro stores no keys. Payments are in rupees, up to ₹5,00,000.

Then, in a chat with a lead who has a sent invoice, press the shopping bag, choose **Payment request**, pick the invoice and read what it will ask: it shows the amount still due. Nothing is sent until you press **Send payment request**. The customer sees Meta's **Review and Pay**. Only one request can be open for an invoice; cancel it (the assistant can send an order status message of **canceled**) before asking again.

You can also send the customer order status messages (processing, shipped, completed, canceled) for a request, through the assistant after you approve. A paid request cannot be canceled.

When WhatsApp reports the payment, Marro writes a note on the lead saying it was confirmed (or failed). **It does not mark the invoice paid and never charges anything.** Open the invoice and use **Record payment** once the money has arrived. The assistant also asks for your approval for every payment request, even in Auto.
