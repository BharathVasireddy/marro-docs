---
title: Customize future payslips
description: Choose how payslips look without changing saved salary figures.
---

Payroll managers can set how payslips look. Open **Team HR → Payroll → Payslip**. Salary figures and calculations do not change: payroll stays in INR and deductions stay manual.

## What you can set

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Wording" icon="type">
    Template name, payslip title, plain-text header and footer, section labels.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Layout" icon="layout">
    Compact or comfortable spacing, and whether attendance is shown.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Save and preview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change and preview">
    Preview shows a selected employee's figures in the new design. It saves nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save before approving">
    Save the template **before** you approve a payroll month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If someone saved first">
    Reload and review before saving again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Later template changes only apply to future approvals.

## Who can see payslips

| Who | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Employees | Their own approved payslips |
| Payroll managers | Approved payslips and previews in their organization |

## Download a month

Managers can download a CSV for an approved month: employee identity, INR amounts, gross, deduction and net. Treat it as a confidential report. It is not a payment file.
