---
title: Customize future payslips
description: Choose payslip presentation without changing saved salary figures.
---

<Note>
Payslip customization screens are being prepared. Existing payroll and payslip screens continue to use their current presentation until this flow is integrated and released.
</Note>

Payroll managers can save a payslip presentation for their organization. It includes a template name, payslip title, plain-text header/footer, section labels and compact or comfortable spacing. Attendance display is optional. Payroll continues to use INR and its existing calculations.

Save the template before approving a payroll month. Approval captures the then-current saved template together with the run, and later template changes apply to future approvals. A preview shows a selected employee's draft or approved figures using the proposed formatting; previewing does not save or approve anything. If another manager saves a change first, reload the template and review it again before saving.

Employees can read their own approved payslips. Payroll managers can read approved payslips and preview entries within their current organization. Access depends on current payroll/employee permissions and the Attendance module.

Older approvals without a recorded presentation use standard formatting and state that original approval-time branding was not recorded. Their stored salary and attendance figures remain unchanged. If presentation storage is unavailable, the payslip explicitly says its approval-time branding cannot be verified.

Managers can download a portable CSV for an approved payroll month. It includes employee identity, explicit INR currency and the stored gross, deduction and net amounts. Text fields are protected against spreadsheet formulas. The file is a confidential payroll report; it is not a payment instruction or a claimed Zoho Payroll/RazorpayX import format.

Deductions remain manually entered. This feature adds no statutory calculations, payment processing, bank details or provider setup. Jurisdiction-specific requirements, provider accounts/contracts and legal approval must be handled separately.
