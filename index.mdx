The smart CRM every business needs: CRM, calling and inbox in one AI-powered platform. Marro's AI agents reply, call, follow up and update leads on their own, and your team works leads, chats, appointments and payments at [app.marro.si](https://app.marro.si), in the [mobile app](/guides/mobile-app) and on WhatsApp.

## Use Marro

- [Inbox](/guides/inbox): WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, website chat and SMS in one place.
- [Website chat](/guides/website-chat): a chat bubble on your own website.
- [Business knowledge](/guides/business-knowledge): the facts the AI answers from.
- [Assistant](/guides/assistant): ask Marro to find and do your work, on the web, in the app and on WhatsApp.
- [Agents](/guides/agents): AI that plans next steps, analyses calls and keeps conversations going.
- [Automations](/guides/automations), [Appointments and booking](/guides/appointments), [Payments](/guides/payments), [AI calling](/guides/ai-calling).

## Connect to the API

Marro's API lets your own server read and write your organization's leads: a website form that sends enquiries, a sync into a spreadsheet or data warehouse, an ad platform that pushes leads.
